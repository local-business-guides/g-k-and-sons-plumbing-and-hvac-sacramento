What to have ready before plumbing work

The right approach depends on the problem. A repair call may begin with a description of the symptom and an assessment of what is causing it. Maintenance can help address plumbing needs before they turn into a more disruptive repair. An installation may call for a conversation about the space, what you want changed, and how the new work should fit into the property.

A short checklist can make your call more useful:

  • Write down when you first noticed the issue and whether it has changed.
  • Identify the fixture or area involved, and whether other areas are affected.
  • Share any steps already taken or previous repairs you know about.
  • For an installation, explain what you’d like added, replaced, or updated.
  • Keep the area accessible if it’s safe and practical to do so.

Avoid taking apart plumbing or trying a repair you’re unsure about. A temporary fix can make it harder to see what’s going on, and an active leak or worsening problem is a reason to call a professional.
